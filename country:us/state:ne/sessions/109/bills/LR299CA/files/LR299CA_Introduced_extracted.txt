2 IV-29 The power to commute any sentence for an offense against the
3 criminal laws of this state, except treason and cases of impeachment,
4 shall be vested in the Nebraska Board of Commutation. The board shall
5 consist of seven members appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the
6 Legislature. The Governor shall appoint the members on a nonpartisan
7 basis. The board's members shall include individuals with expertise in
8 mental health, restorative justice, criminal law, law enforcement, and
9 lived experience of incarceration and victimization. The Legislature
10 shall provide by law for the board's procedures and its members' terms
11 and qualifications.
