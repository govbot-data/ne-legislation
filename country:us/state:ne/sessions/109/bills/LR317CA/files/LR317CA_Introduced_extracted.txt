1 THE MEMBERS OF THE ONE HUNDRED NINTH LEGISLATURE OF NEBRASKA, SECOND
2 SESSION, RESOLVE THAT:
3 Section 1. At the general election in November 2026, the following
4 proposed amendment to the Constitution of Nebraska shall be submitted to
5 the electors of the State of Nebraska for approval or rejection:
6 To add a new section 14 to Article VIII:
7 VIII-14 (1) Beginning January 1, 2027, the total amount of property
8 taxes that may be levied by a political subdivision in any year shall not
9 exceed the total amount levied in the prior year by more than the
10 allowable growth percentage.
11 (2) For purposes of this section:
12 (a) Allowable growth percentage means a percentage equal to the sum
13 of (i) two percent plus (ii) the political subdivision's real growth
14 percentage;
15 (b) Real growth percentage means the percentage obtained by dividing
16 (i) the political subdivision's real growth value by (ii) the political
17 subdivision's total property valuation from the prior year; and
18 (c) Real growth value means the increase in a political
19 subdivision's total property valuation from the prior year to the current
20 year due to (i) improvements to real property as a result of new
21 construction and additions to existing buildings, (ii) any other
22 improvements to real property which increase the value of such property,
23 (iii) annexation of real property by the political subdivision, (iv) a
