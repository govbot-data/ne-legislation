WHEREAS, Ken Lonowski of Waverly, Nebraska, has displayed tremendous
public service during his life; and
WHEREAS, Ken served for twenty-eight years in the United States Army and
Nebraska Army National Guard; and
WHEREAS, Ken received a Presidential unit citation and numerous service
medals and awards; and
WHEREAS, Ken served in the Eighty-second Airborne and was named
Paratrooper of the Year in 1995; and
WHEREAS, Ken moved to a military intelligence unit and was selected as one
of only fifty-four in the United States Military for a team that specialized in
weapons of mass destruction and terrorist activities; and
WHEREAS, Ken retired from the United States Army in 2011 and was hired by
the Nebraska State Patrol for a security position in the Nebraska State Capitol
on March 21, 2016; and
WHEREAS, Ken has served as a Security Guard, in Security Communications,
and as Shift Supervisor for the Nebraska State Patrol; and
WHEREAS, Ken has set the standard for others on the Capitol Security team
to follow and he was named Supervisor of the Year in 2021; and
WHEREAS, Ken loves working with others and bringing smiles to people's
faces; and
WHEREAS, Ken retired on January 9, 2026; and
WHEREAS, Ken and his wife, Chris, have four children, Kalvin, Tony,
Ashley, and Mikayla, as well as several grandchildren.
