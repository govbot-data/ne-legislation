ONE HUNDRED NINTH LEGISLATURE
SECOND SESSION
LEGISLATIVE RESOLUTION 305CA
PROPOSED CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT
Introduced by Lippincott, 34; Hansen, 16; Holdcroft, 36; Murman, 38.
Read first time January 16, 2026
Committee: Government, Military and Veterans Affairs
1 THE MEMBERS OF THE ONE HUNDRED NINTH LEGISLATURE OF NEBRASKA, SECOND
2 SESSION, RESOLVE THAT:
3 Section 1. At the general election in November 2026, the following
4 proposed amendment to the Constitution of Nebraska shall be submitted to
5 the electors of the State of Nebraska for approval or rejection:
6 To add a new section 12 to Article XVII:
7 XVII-12 The Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, State
8 Treasurer, Attorney General, or Auditor of Public Accounts or any member
9 of the Legislature may be recalled from office through the procedure and
10 in the manner provided for by law. The procedure shall be known as recall
11 and shall be in addition to any other method of removal from office
12 provided by this Constitution.
13 Sec. 2. The proposed amendment shall be submitted to the electors
14 in the manner prescribed by the Constitution of Nebraska, Article XVI,
15 section 1, with the following ballot language:
16 A constitutional amendment to provide for recall of the Governor,
17 Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, State Treasurer, Attorney
18 General, Auditor of Public Accounts, and members of the Legislature as
19 provided by law.
20 For
21 Against.
