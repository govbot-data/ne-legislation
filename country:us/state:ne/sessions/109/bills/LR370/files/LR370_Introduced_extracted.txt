ONE HUNDRED NINTH LEGISLATURE
SECOND SESSION
LEGISLATIVE RESOLUTION 370
Introduced by DeBoer, 10.
WHEREAS, Brayden Jensen, Hagan Jones, and Jared Pacha, members of Troop
308 in Omaha, Nebraska, have completed the requirements for the rank of Eagle
Scout in Scouting America; and
WHEREAS, to earn the rank of Eagle Scout, the highest rank in scouting, a
scout must fulfill requirements in the areas of leadership, service, and
outdoor skills; and
WHEREAS, throughout their scouting experience, Brayden, Hagan, and Jared
have learned, been tested on, and been recognized for various scouting skills;
and
WHEREAS, to achieve the rank of Eagle Scout, a scout is required to earn
twenty-one merit badges, fourteen of which must be in required areas, serve in
a scout leadership position, and complete a community service project; and
WHEREAS, for his Eagle Scout community service project, Brayden designed
and built large shelves for Nebraska Animal Loving Advocates to better store
and organize large bags of product; and
WHEREAS, for his Eagle Scout community service project, Hagan designed and
built new and replacement bookshelves for the Children's Center at the Open
Door Mission; and
WHEREAS, for his Eagle Scout community service project, Jared designed,
built, and installed bat boxes for the Raptor Conservation Alliance; and
WHEREAS, only a small percentage of the scouts who join Scouting America
achieve the rank of Eagle Scout; and
WHEREAS, through their dedication to excellence Brayden, Hagan, and Jared
have joined other high achievers who are Eagle Scouts, including astronauts,
political and industry leaders, artists, scientists, and athletes.
