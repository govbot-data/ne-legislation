3 Section 1. At the general election in November 2026, the following
4 proposed amendment to the Constitution of Nebraska shall be submitted to
5 the electors of the State of Nebraska for approval or rejection:
6 To add a new section 31 to Article III:
7 III-31 In addition to any other requirements of this Constitution,
8 in establishing or revising the boundaries of all election districts for
9 federal and state offices as provided in the Constitution of the United
10 States or this Constitution:
11 (1) Every ten years beginning in 2031, the Legislature shall
12 establish a screening committee of nine of its members, no more than five
13 of whom may be affiliated with the same political party. Action by the
14 screening committee shall require the affirmative votes of at least six
15 of its members. The screening committee shall appoint a Commission on
16 Redistricting with responsibility to draw and recommend to the
17 Legislature the boundaries of election districts;
18 (2) The commission shall be comprised of nine registered voters of
19 the State of Nebraska divided into three groups: Three commissioners
20 affiliated with the largest political party in the state, three
21 commissioners affiliated with the second largest political party in the
22 state, and three commissioners not affiliated with either of these
23 political parties. The opportunity to serve on the commission shall be

3 (10) Subject to other provisions of this Constitution, election
4 districts shall be drawn in accordance with the following criteria, in
5 order of priority: (a) Complying with federal law, (b) creating
6 contiguous districts, (c) making districts as nearly equal in population

8 to vote on the basis of race or language, (e) minimizing the division of

23 (14) All proceedings of the Legislature and its committees regarding
