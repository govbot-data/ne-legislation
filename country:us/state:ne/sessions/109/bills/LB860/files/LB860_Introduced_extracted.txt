1 Section 1. Section 71-801, Revised Statutes Cumulative Supplement,
2 2024, is amended to read:
3 71-801 Sections 71-801 to 71-830, sections 2 to 3 of this act, and
4 the Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic Act shall be known and
5 may be cited as the Nebraska Behavioral Health Services Act.
6 Sec. 2. (1) No later than January 1, 2027, the Department of Health
7 and Human Services shall establish a program that provides access and
8 services for individuals under twenty-one years of age who are
9 experiencing complex behavioral health care needs.
10 (2) The department shall ensure that: (a) The program addresses the
11 behavioral health care of individuals under twenty-one years of age who
12 have complex, multisystem needs, with the goals of supporting families in
13 the community and preventing institutionalization and out-of-home care
14 placement; and (b) care management under the program is delivered
15 according to the needs of the individual and the individual's family.
16 Sec. 3. (1) The department shall provide the following services
17 within the program described in section 2 of this act, including, but not
18 limited to:
19 (a) Mobile response and stabilization;
20 (b) Intensive home-based treatment;
21 (c) Inpatient and outpatient care;
22 (d) Psychiatric residential treatment facility care;
23 (e) Substance use disorder treatment;
24 (f) Psychiatric care; and
25 (g) Developmental disability care.
26 (2) Care coordination shall be provided through the behavioral
27 health regions, as determined by the department. The department may
28 procure a single, statewide managed care plan that builds a sufficient
29 provider network to ensure access to behavioral health services,
30 including residential treatment and home and community-based services, in
31 order to provide a full continuum of behavioral health care. The
